# JNU OSE Free Trial Onboarding & Private Evidence Boundary — 2026-09-08

The OSE Free Trial is not yet production-ready for JNU.

Key findings from current OSE materials:

- Free Trial is one month in principle and limited to internal usage before an Information Provision Agreement.
- The published operational process describes the Applicant as a company and requires company/contact/representative details; individual eligibility is not publicly established.
- The linked current application form is company-oriented and does not explicitly list Nikkei 225 micro Futures. It contains an Other product field, but the project will not assume that is sufficient without OSE confirmation.
- Direct OSE connectivity is not suitable for an immediate cloud/mobile first-real path because published procedures require legal-entity documentation/network diagrams and additional Market Data User ID, network and TMI connection processes with multi-month lead times.
- The preferred trial route, if approved, is indirect delivery through an OSE-contracted Direct User/provider.
- The Free Trial makes OSE information available free of charge in principle, but no public source establishes that a third-party provider or transport fee is also waived.
- Free Trial data is internal-use data. The current JNU repository is public; therefore raw entitled quote evidence must not be committed to it.
- Public cryptographic hashes, forecasts or other derived outputs are not presumed permitted. OSE's current policy defines approved Derived Data narrowly and requires OSE approval plus non-reconstructibility / specific expertise conditions.
- Private third-party cloud processing is also not presumed permitted until OSE/provider confirms the applicable Service Facilitator/internal-use treatment.

The new storage boundary is fail-closed. It permits raw entitled data only in private internal storage and rejects public raw destinations, unapproved cloud processing, unapproved public hash/derived publication, and reconstructive fields in public outputs.
