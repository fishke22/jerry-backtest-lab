## Related Japanese evidence

A KAKEN research project led by Hiroshi Moriyasu on economic-policy uncertainty and the Japanese economy reports that higher policy uncertainty is associated with stronger stock-price commonality and lower stock-market liquidity/commonality. Related 2025 JSRI work reports that higher EPU increases stock-price synchronicity, with trade-policy and FX-policy uncertainty among important categories.

Sources:
- https://kaken.nii.ac.jp/ja/grant/KAKENHI-PROJECT-20H01499/
- https://www.jsri.or.jp/publication/periodical/economics/132/132-02/

## JNU interpretation

Do not convert EPU into a bullish/bearish vote.

The useful JNU hypothesis is a **liquidity/evidence-quality regime**:

- elevated policy uncertainty may change depth, spreads, price impact, co-movement and the reliability of ordinary technical signals;
- trade/tariff or FX-policy uncertainty may matter more than a generic headline count;
- a breakout/reversal observed under stressed liquidity should not automatically be treated the same as one observed under normal liquidity.

## Frozen research tests

1. Test EPU regime against Nikkei futures liquidity/illiquidity measures.
2. Control for VIX, realized volatility, scheduled macro events and time-of-day.
3. Separate trade/tariff and FX-policy uncertainty where reproducible subindices are available.
4. Test whether EPU adds information to liquidity/risk classification before testing any trading EV.
5. Do not infer JNU direction from high EPU alone.
